About Me

I’ve been an avid runner since 2012 and have completed 33 marathons and ultras to date and over 200 parkruns.

Other than being a complete running nut I really enjoy bodyweight exercises and functional movement. I’m very passionate about retaining mobility and flexibility as we get older — I understand some of the noises we make getting out of a chair!

What Can I Do For You?

If you’re looking to complete your first 5km, 10km, half marathon, marathon or even an ultra — I’ve got you covered.

So how does it work? I offer 1-2-1 personal training — we start with a consultation where we can chat and figure out the best path for you. From here I can create bespoke training plans, workouts, food diary analysis, nutrition guidance — whatever you need to get you on the path towards your goals.
